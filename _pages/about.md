I am an Assistant Professor at the **State Key Laboratory of Multimodal Artificial Intelligence Systems, Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA)**.

I received my B.Eng. degree in Automation from Central South University and my Ph.D. degree in Control Theory and Control Engineering from CASIA, advised by [Prof. Shuo Wang](https://ia.cas.cn/rcdw/yjy/202404/t20240425_7131998.html). I collaborate closely with [Prof. Shaowei Cui](https://shaoweicui.com/). My research focuses on **visuotactile sensing**, **tactile simulation**, **dexterous robotic manipulation**, and **tactile foundation models**.
